Verification Notes

  • MLB 2027: MLB.com announced 2027 regular season key dates (Opening Night Mar 24, Traditional Opening Day Mar 25, first full slate Mar 27, All-Star Jul 13, HR Derby Jul 12, second half Jul 15, regular season ends Sep 26). Game-level times are not all published; the calendar treats the season as a busy MLB window and labels day-level precision accordingly.
  • MLB 2028-2029: No official game-by-game schedules released at time of generation. Season windows are estimated from typical MLB calendar patterns and must be reverified when MLB publishes official schedules.
  • NFL 2026-27: NFL.com important dates page provides verified 2026-27 key dates including Super Bowl LXI (Feb 14, 2027). Westwood One NFL schedule page provides verified national radio broadcast listings for remaining 2026 season games shown on the page.
  • NFL 2027-28/2028-29/2029-30: Full official schedules not released. Super Bowl LXII date (Feb 13, 2028, Atlanta) is supported by NFL announcement reporting; other dates are estimated from NFL scheduling patterns (Thu after Labor Day kickoff, 18-week regular season, postseason into early/mid Feb). Marked estimated.
  • Westwood One: Used as official broadcaster source for national NFL radio packages. Station-level SF AM/FM availability for specific affiliates was not fully verified line-by-line from an official station-finder lookup in this run; flagged for review. Westwood One package scope (TNF/SNF/MNF/Thanksgiving/holiday/playoffs) is evidenced by official schedule page.
  • Stanford/Cal 2026: Official athletic site schedules used. Some times TBA on official schedules; left as TBA.
  • Stanford/Cal 2027-2029: No official schedules released. Season windows estimated from typical late-Aug through late-Nov college football regular season; flagged.
  • Average game durations: Estimated planning assumptions (MLB ~165 min, NFL ~195 min, NCAAF ~210 min). Not official schedule facts; labeled estimated.
