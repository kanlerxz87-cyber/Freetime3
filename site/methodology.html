Definition of "free"

A minute is free when none of these are on air:

  • MLB — all 30 clubs, regular season + postseason (Giants & A's flagged HIGH PRIORITY)
  • NFL — all games, preseason → Super Bowl (49ers HIGH PRIORITY)
  • NCAAF — Stanford & Cal HIGH PRIORITY
  • Westwood One national NFL radio broadcasts

All times Pacific (PT). Game durations use published averages (MLB 2h38m, NFL/NCAAF 3h12m) — see Radio Guide.
