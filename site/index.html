✅ The one-line answer

Best vacation window: mid-February → late March every year.

Between the Super Bowl (mid-February) and MLB Opening Day (late March) there is a 5–6 week stretch with zero MLB, NFL, Stanford, or Cal games on the air. That is the only multi-week window that exists. Every other week of the year has at least one game in the tracked set.

🔴 What counts as "busy"

  • MLB — all 30 clubs (Giants & A's HIGH PRIORITY)
  • NFL — all games incl. 49ers HIGH PRIORITY, preseason → Super Bowl
  • NCAAF — Stanford & Cal HIGH PRIORITY
  • Westwood One national NFL radio (TNF/SNF/MNF)

A minute is free only when none of these are on air. See Methodology.
