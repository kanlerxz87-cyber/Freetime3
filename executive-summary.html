Bottom line up front

There is exactly one multi-week vacation window per year: mid-February through late March. It starts the day after the Super Bowl and ends the day before MLB Opening Day. Everything else on the calendar has at least one tracked game on the air.

Why there's no other window

🔴 Mar 25 – Oct 31: MLB regular season + postseason (every day has games; ~162 games/team).
🔴 Aug 6 – Feb 14: NFL preseason → Super Bowl (incl. Westwood One national radio every Thu/Sun/Mon night).
🔴 Late Aug – late Nov: Stanford + Cal college football (Saturdays, overlapping both above).
🟢 Mid-Feb – late Mar: nothing — NFL is over, MLB hasn't started, NCAAF is months away. This is the only gap.

The three busy blocks overlap so heavily that they merge into one continuous ~327-day busy block per year. The free gap is what's left between blocks.

High-priority flags

Giants, Athletics, 49ers, Stanford, and Cal are flagged HIGH PRIORITY throughout — but the "free" test counts all 30 MLB clubs and all NFL games, not just the flagged ones. If any game in the full set is on, the minute is busy.

What to do next

  • Book inside the February–March window.
  • Re-check this site after MLB/NFL release their 2028 schedules (expected mid-2027) — estimates will be replaced with verified dates.
  • Use the Day Scoreboard to confirm any specific day before booking.
